About Stone Trend

Canadian Stone Specialists For Performance-Driven Spaces.

Stone Trend is a countertop fabrication and installation company focused on high-performing stone solutions for residential and commercial environments across Canada. From slab selection through final sign-off, we deliver disciplined project management with meticulous craft, standardized quality controls, and a clear process: templating, digital layout, CNC fabrication, hand finishing, delivery, and installation.

Mission

To be the stone partner of choice for builders, designers, and owners by delivering reliable lead times, precise installations, and surfaces that elevate every project.

Why Choose Stone Trend

  • Integrated residential and commercial capabilities
  • Established relationships with major slab suppliers
  • Digitized shop drawings and approvals
  • Experienced installation crews and site supervisors

Our Services

Residential Projects

Natural and engineered stone fabrication for custom homes, renovations, and multi-unit residences.

  • Kitchen surfaces and waterfall islands
  • Fireplace surrounds and accent walls

Commercial Projects

Stone fabrication and installation for hospitality, retail, office, institutional, and mixed-use developments.

  • Restaurant kitchens and bar installations
  • Lobbies, reception desks, and feature walls
  • Amenity and back-of-house areas
  • Phased and multi-site installation programs
