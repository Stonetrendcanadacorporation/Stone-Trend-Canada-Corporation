Granite Slab Catalogue

Granite Slabs

Granite is a natural igneous stone quarried in large slabs — every piece has authentic grain, flecks, and movement that cannot be replicated by engineered surfaces. It is exceptionally hard and heat-resistant, making it a proven choice for kitchen countertops, outdoor kitchens, and bar tops where durability matters.

Because granite is natural stone, it is porous and benefits from periodic sealing, but it often delivers strong value — lasting performance and distinctive character at a cost that can undercut premium marble or quartzite. Granite remains one of the most specified natural stones on Stone Trend residential and commercial projects across the GTA.

This catalogue includes granite slabs and tiles. Exact colours, finishes, and inventory are confirmed during project pricing.
