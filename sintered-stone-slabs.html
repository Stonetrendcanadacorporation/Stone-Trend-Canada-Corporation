Sintered Stone Catalogue

Large-Format Sintered Stone Slabs.

Sintered stone is an ultra-compact engineered surface made from natural minerals fused under extreme heat and pressure. It delivers marble- and stone-look aesthetics in large-format slabs with outstanding durability for countertops, vanities, cladding, and feature walls.

Sintered stone vs. quartz

Both are engineered surfaces, but they are made differently. Quartz combines ground quartz with polymer resins for a non-porous, low-maintenance countertop. Sintered stone is resin-free: minerals are sintered into a dense slab with very high heat, scratch, and UV resistance.

  • Composition: Sintered stone is mineral-based with no resin; quartz typically contains resin binders.
  • Heat resistance: Sintered stone tolerates higher temperatures; quartz can be affected by extreme heat because of resin content.
  • Format: Sintered stone is often supplied in large thin slabs (e.g. 1600×3200 mm at 12 mm); quartz is commonly 2 cm or 3 cm.
  • Applications: Both suit kitchens and bathrooms; sintered stone is also well suited to cladding and areas needing UV stability.
