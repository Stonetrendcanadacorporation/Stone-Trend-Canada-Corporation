Marble Slab Catalogue

Marble Slabs

Marble is a natural metamorphic stone prized for soft veining, luminous depth, and timeless luxury — the same material found in historic architecture and high-end interiors worldwide. Each slab is genuinely one-of-a-kind; vein direction, tone, and movement vary in ways engineered stone cannot duplicate.

Marble is softer and more porous than granite or quartzite, so sealing and thoughtful placement matter — it shines in bathroom vanities, feature walls, and statement islands where beauty is the priority. It is a premium natural stone, not the most abrasion-resistant option for every kitchen, but nothing matches its classic elegance.

The slabs shown here represent the range of marble colours and movements available through Stone Trend. Exact veining and availability are reviewed during selection.
