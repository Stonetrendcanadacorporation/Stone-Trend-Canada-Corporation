Onyx Gemstone Catalogue

Onyx Gemstone Slabs

Onyx and gemstone slabs are among the most dramatic natural surfaces available — often translucent, vividly coloured, and banded in ways that make them ideal for backlit feature walls, luxury vanities, and statement bar tops. This is real natural stone with jewel-like depth, not an engineered or printed surface.

Onyx is chosen for visual impact and artistry rather than heavy-duty abrasion resistance; it is typically softer and more delicate than granite or quartzite and benefits from careful sealing and thoughtful placement. Each slab is unique — rose quartz, electra gems, and exotic crystal tones are sought after in high-end residential and hospitality design.

Collection sourced from Pandora Porcelain and Quartz. Slab sizes, finishes, and availability are confirmed at quotation.
