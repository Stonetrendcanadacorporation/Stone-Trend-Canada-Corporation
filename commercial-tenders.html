Bidding & Tenders

Commercial Tenders

Stone Trend participates in commercial tender processes for residential, hospitality, and mixed-use construction projects throughout the GTA and nearby regions.

We work with developers, general contractors, millwork contractors, and project teams to provide detailed quotations for stone fabrication and installation. Our tender support is structured to align with procurement timelines, material coordination, and active construction schedules.

Tender Support for Commercial Projects

Our team provides pricing and project coordination for countertop packages and surface installations across a range of commercial environments. We understand the importance of scheduling, consistency, and clear communication during the tender stage, particularly for multi-unit and phased construction.

Stone Trend supports project teams seeking a reliable trade partner for stone fabrication and installation, whether for a single commercial interior or a larger residential development.

Typical Tender Scope

  • Multi-unit residential kitchens
  • Bathroom vanities
  • Hospitality and restaurant surfaces
  • Lobby and amenity countertops
  • Custom millwork countertops
  • Commercial interior stone surfaces

What We Provide

  • Project-based quotations
  • Material sourcing and coordination
  • Fabrication planning
  • Installation scheduling
  • Coordination with contractors, designers, and millwork teams
  • Support for commercial and multi-phase projects

Who We Work With

Stone Trend works with developers, general contractors, millwork contractors, designers, and project managers seeking stone solutions for residential and commercial projects. Our team is available to review tender packages, project drawings, and specifications as part of the quotation process.

Project Types

  • Condominiums and apartment buildings
  • Hospitality and hotel interiors
  • Restaurants and amenity spaces
  • Mixed-use developments
  • Residential developments
  • Commercial fit-outs and interior projects
