Quartzite Slab Catalogue

Quartzite Slabs

Quartzite is a natural stone formed when sandstone is compressed and heated deep in the earth — harder than granite and often mistaken for marble because of its elegant veining. It delivers marble-like beauty with far greater scratch and heat resistance, making it one of the top natural choices for busy kitchen countertops and waterfall islands.

Every quartzite slab is unique, with natural variation in colour and movement. It is a premium natural stone — typically priced above granite and engineered quartz, but valued for authenticity and long-term durability. Sealing is recommended, as with most natural stones.

The quartzite slabs in this catalogue are among the natural options Stone Trend supplies for countertops, vanities, and high-traffic surfaces across Ontario.
