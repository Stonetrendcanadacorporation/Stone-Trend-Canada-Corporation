Travertine Slabs

Travertine is a natural sedimentary limestone formed by mineral-rich springs, recognized by warm earthy tones and a distinctive pitted, porous texture. It has been used for thousands of years in Mediterranean architecture and remains popular for floors, bathroom walls, shower surrounds, and pool coping where a relaxed, organic feel is desired.

Travertine is softer than granite or quartzite and is naturally porous, so sealing is recommended — especially in wet areas. It is not an engineered surface; each slab carries authentic geological character rather than uniform manufactured patterning.

This catalogue highlights travertine slabs specified on Stone Trend residential and hospitality projects. Final selections, finishes, and inventory are confirmed during pricing.
