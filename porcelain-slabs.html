Porcelain Slabs and Tiles Catalogue

Porcelain Slabs and Tiles

Porcelain slabs and tiles are an engineered ceramic surface fired at extreme temperature — not natural stone, but a highly durable, low-maintenance alternative that can mimic marble, concrete, and stone with remarkable realism. Large-format porcelain slabs are increasingly popular for kitchen countertops, shower walls, and cladding because they resist scratches, stains, and moisture.

Porcelain is heat-tolerant and UV-stable and is often more affordable than natural stone at a similar visual scale, though thin large slabs require experienced fabrication. Unlike quartz, porcelain contains no resin binders — it is pure sintered ceramic.

This catalogue includes porcelain slabs and tiles from multiple Olympia collections (Quebec, Aequa, Antikas, Arkipro, Arkistone, and more). Use the Type filter for slabs only or tiles only. Exact colours, finishes, and availability are confirmed with our team.
