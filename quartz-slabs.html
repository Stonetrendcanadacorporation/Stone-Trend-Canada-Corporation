Quartz Slab Catalogue

Quartz Slabs

Quartz is an engineered stone — roughly 90% natural quartz crystals combined with resins and pigments, manufactured for consistent colour and pattern rather than mined as a single slab. It is one of the most popular and practical countertop choices in Ontario kitchens and bathrooms because it is non-porous, stain-resistant, and low maintenance compared with natural stone.

Quartz handles everyday cooking and cleaning well, though sudden extreme heat can stress resin binders — trivets are still recommended. This catalogue combines Olympia Tile quartz with the Pandora Porcelain and Quartz collection (Aura, Luminor, Forma, and Mono series), offering a wide range of marble-look veining, concrete tones, and bold colours at different price points.

Pandora colours are sourced from Pandora Porcelain and Quartz. Final slab availability, patterning, and sizing are confirmed at quotation.
