Fireclay Sinks

#401 Fireclay

Apron/Textured Sink. Fireclay apron sink (also known as a farmhouse sink) crafted from natural clay blended with fine-grained micas and quartz, finished with a durable enamel glaze, and high-temperature fired for a smooth, glossy, long-wearing surface. Designed as a timeless kitchen centrepiece for stone countertop installations. Contact Stone Trend for availability and project pricing. What is fireclay? Unlike stainless steel or granite composite, fireclay sinks are made from natural clay minerals that are moulded, glazed, and fired at extremely high heat. The result is a dense, heavy-duty basin with a hard, non-porous ceramic-style finish that resists staining and keeps its character for years. Apron-front models expose the sink face below the countertop for a classic farmhouse look.
