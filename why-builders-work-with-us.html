Why Builders Work With Us

Builders, developers, and general contractors across Ontario work with Stone Trend for natural and engineered stone fabrication and installation. Our team follows structured fabrication processes and organized installation coordination suited for residential and commercial project environments.

Structured Fabrication Process

From digital templating and CNC cutting to polishing and finishing, each step follows a defined workflow intended to maintain consistent quality and production timelines.

Installation Coordination

Installation is coordinated with project schedules and other trades so stone surfaces can be delivered and installed when the site is ready.

Experience Across Residential And Commercial

Our team has delivered stone fabrication and installation across single-family homes, multi-unit developments, hospitality spaces, retail environments, and institutional projects.

Material Sourcing And Inventory Availability

Access to quartz, granite, marble, and porcelain through in-house inventory and established supplier networks.

Installation And Finishing Standards

Installation crews focus on seam work, edge details, and general site cleanliness to support smooth project handoff to other trades.

Scheduling And Trade Coordination

Project timelines and installation planning are coordinated with contractors and trades to align with overall construction schedules.

Project Scope Flexibility

Stone fabrication and installation can be adapted to support a range of project sizes, from custom residential builds to multi-unit developments and commercial interiors.

Custom Fabrication Capabilities

Stone Trend supports a variety of fabrication requirements including specialty edge profiles, waterfall installations, feature surfaces, and custom stone elements.

Stone Trend focuses on consistent workmanship and organized coordination aligned with project scope. Whether supplying stone packages for multi-unit developments or custom surfaces for residential and commercial environments, our team supports builders and developers from project inquiry through installation across Ontario and surrounding regions.
